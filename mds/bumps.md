# Bumps

Bumps are small raised areas that appear on a surface. They can occur naturally or develop as a result of external factors. In humans, bumps commonly appear on the skin due to conditions such as acne, insect bites, allergic reactions, irritation, infections, or ingrown hairs.

Skin bumps may vary in size, shape, colour, and texture. Some are painless and harmless, while others may be itchy, tender, or indicate an underlying medical condition. Proper skincare, hygiene, and medical treatment when necessary can help manage or prevent certain types of bumps.

Beyond medicine, the term "bumps" can also refer to raised irregularities on roads, objects, materials, or natural landscapes. For example, speed bumps are elevated sections of roads designed to slow down vehicles, while bumps on surfaces may result from manufacturing processes, wear, or environmental effects.

The appearance and significance of bumps depend largely on their cause, location, and characteristics.
